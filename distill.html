Architecture & Performance

Engineered for Verifiable Correctness

WhyCode replaces fragile generative chat wrappers with a high-throughput, deterministic dual-process pipeline.

System 1 Intent Router & Abstention Gate

Non-autoregressive keyword & pattern classification running in 12 microseconds. Implements conformal prediction to immediately refuse ungrounded queries before touching the graph.

System 2 Dual Graph Store (SQLite + Neo4j)

Executes 2-hop bidirectional BFS traversals to assemble multi-hop evidence chains. Serves answers from memory in 0.5ms (1,500x faster than baseline) with visible Cypher queries.
